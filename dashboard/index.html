How the picks are made
Model
Team offense, defense and pace ratings refit before every game date, plus a machine-learning correction, projecting each game's total score.
Bet rule
Bet the over or under against the opening total when the calibrated model probability is at or above the cutoff above.
Backtest
On 2024–26 opening totals, this rule won about 55% of bets. Past results do not guarantee future ones.
Lines
From ESPN's odds feed: the median opening total across sportsbooks, and the line posted when the pick was logged.
Not included
Spread picks. The model showed no edge on spreads, so they are not played.
