How the picks are made
Model
Offense, defense and pace ratings for every team, refit before each game date, plus a machine-learning correction. It projects each game's total points.
Bet rule
Over or under the opening total when the model's calibrated confidence is at least —. Everything else is a pass.
Backtest
This rule won about 55% of bets on 2024–26 opening totals and about 57% on 2014–21. Past results do not guarantee future ones.
Lines
ESPN's odds feed: the median opening total across sportsbooks, and the line posted when the pick was logged.
Profit
Units at the posted price, or -110 when none was posted. "At pick-time lines" grades against the number you could have bet that morning.
Spreads
Not played. The model showed no edge against the spread.
